Investment Thesis

Where We Deploy Capital

We don't buy stabilized assets at full price. We buy operational problems we know how to fix — in markets where new supply can't enter.

01
Distressed & Value-Add
We acquire hotels trading below replacement cost where the current operator cannot or will not capture the available upside. We buy broken operations and fix them. The basis is where returns are made — not the renovation plan.
02
High Barrier-to-Entry Markets
Supply-constrained markets where new hotel development is economically or regulatorily prohibitive. When new supply cannot enter, existing assets sustain ADR and occupancy through any market cycle. Scarcity is a structural moat.
03
Owner-Operator Advantage
We self-manage every asset we own. No third-party management overhead. No absentee decisions. We walk our properties, know our GMs by name, and respond to operational problems in hours — not weeks.

Astera Hospitality Group is a privately held hotel investment firm founded by a second-generation hotel operating family. We acquire, renovate, and operate branded hotels across the Southeast and Mid-Atlantic, and offer accredited investors passive income through structured private placements under SEC Regulation D.
Our investment opportunities are available exclusively to verified accredited investors under SEC Regulation D Rule 506(b). To qualify, you must have annual income of $200,000+ individually ($300,000 jointly) for the past two years, or a net worth exceeding $1 million excluding your primary residence. Minimum investment is $50,000.
Distributions are paid quarterly — in January, April, July, and October. The 8% preferred return is cumulative: if a quarter is short due to a renovation or slow period, the unpaid amount accrues and must be paid in full before any other distributions are made. You earn first.
Hotel syndications offer significant tax advantages. Cost segregation studies on renovation spend can generate substantial Year 1 paper losses — potentially $40,000+ per $100K invested — which can offset passive income on your tax return. You'll receive a Schedule K-1 annually. Consult your CPA for how these benefits apply to your specific situation.
Yes. Accredited investors can participate through a self-directed IRA or qualified retirement account. You'll need to work with your IRA custodian to confirm eligibility and coordinate the transfer. Our team can help guide you through the process.
Each deal has a defined 5–7 year hold period with a clear exit strategy — either a sale or refinancing. This is an illiquid investment and early redemption is not guaranteed. After Year 3, if you've achieved your target return, Astera has the right (but not the obligation) to buy out your interest through our GP Call Option.
Private placements are illiquid investments. There is no guaranteed early exit. In exceptional personal circumstances, transfer of your interest may be possible with GP consent. We encourage investors to only commit capital they can hold for the full term. This is covered in full in the PPM.
